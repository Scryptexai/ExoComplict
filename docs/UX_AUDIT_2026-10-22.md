# Audit — EXOCOMFLICT: Stellar Horizon, 2026-10-22

Checkpoint A of the visual-coherence pass. Nothing here is changed yet; this is
what the build actually is, measured from the shipped payload and read off the
frames in `exports/flow/before/` (16 frames, 390x844 and 1280x720, captured by
`tools/qa_flow_shots.mjs`; regenerate with
`node tools/qa_flow_shots.mjs --tag before`).

The rule for this document: a screen that has not been opened in the browser is
not described here. Everything below was reached by the real journey or by the
`?qa_stage=` deep link into the same `FrontEnd`, and every frame passed the
blank/render check in `tools/image_stats.py`.

## 3. Data honesty — there is no fake stat

This was checked because a redesign is the easiest place to invent one.

| Screen | Data | Source | Fake? |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| DASHBOARD | `BATTLE POINTS`, `BEST SCORE`, `RUNS COMPLETED`, `LOADOUT` | `ProgressionService` profile | no |
| WEAPON | `DMG / RATE / MAG / RELOAD / RNG / SPLASH`, `LOCKED · 120 BP` | `WeaponDefinition` `.tres` at runtime; cost from `ProgressionService.COSTS` | no |
| HERO | name, blurb, `STATUS FIELD READY` | `HeroDefinition` `.tres` | no — and deliberately **no stats**, because no hero stat model exists |
| WORLD | name, blurb, `STATUS OPEN` | `WorldDefinition` `.tres` | no |

**Finding that matters:** hero identity has **no gameplay effect at all** — name
and portrait only, by design, because `HeroDefinition` carries no stats. The
selection flow "saves" a hero id and the save round-trips, but nothing in combat
reads it. That is honest today only because the hero card claims no stats; the
moment the card shows a number, it becomes fake data. Recorded, not fixed here.

Both stages used `loading_keyart.jpg` as the backdrop, with the robot only a
160 px element in one corner, so they looked like one screen.

**Correction, after fixing the instrument (see §7):** the pair of frames the
first capture filed as LOADING and CINEMATIC were both of *CINEMATIC*. The tool
waited the full settle delay after the stage announced itself, and in a web
build a load finishes in one frame, so the front end had already advanced. The
capture now photographs transient stages the moment they are drawn and reports
`advancedTo` when the stage left before the shutter. The defect is real for a
different reason: the two stages shared a composition, which is what a slow load
-- the case the screen exists for -- would have shown.

## 5. What the frames actually look like

Turned into a list of defects, ordered by how much they break coherence.

### 5.1 The world does not belong to the same game as the menus

| | Menus | Gameplay |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Reference | `dashboard_bg.jpg`, `loading_keyart.jpg`: near-black navy, cyan light sources, amber sodium accents, volumetric dust | `tour-2-390x844.png`, `gameplay-390x844.png` |
| Measured | dark, high contrast, small cyan/amber highlights | **bright, low contrast, pale blue-grey** |
| Cause | n/a | separate hard-coded palette, never joined to `AegisTokens` |

The gameplay palette lives in two dicts — `scifi_kit_library.COLORS` (FBX kit)
and `neon_frontier_world._create_materials()` (procedural structures) — and
neither references the design tokens the entire UI is built from. Concretely:

* `COLORS.steel = (0.34, 0.40, 0.45)` and `COLORS.steel_light = (0.43, 0.49, 0.53)`
  are *lighter* than the UI's mid-grey, on large surfaces that fill the screen.
* `POLICY` paints **every wall surface `orange`** (`(0.82, 0.35, 0.14)`), so the
  arena's perimeter is a saturated orange run, not structure with hazard trim.
* `_materials.pearl = (0.58, 0.65, 0.70)` — the brightest value in the game — is
  the 38 m arena deck, i.e. the largest single surface in view.
* Lighting adds to it: sun `energy 0.68` warm, ambient `(0.40, 0.46, 0.52)` at
  `0.49`, exposure `1.01`, contrast `1.09`. A bright ambient on a bright albedo.

### 5.2 Dashboard hierarchy is inverted

Required order is hero → mission → DEPLOY → profile → resources → secondary.
What ships (`dashboard-390x844.png`):

1. wordmark (2. profile block: `BATTLE POINTS / BEST SCORE / RUNS / LOADOUT`)
3. `ARMORY` `SETTINGS` 4. `REPLAY INTRO` 5. **`DEPLOY` at the very bottom**

The hero is not on the screen at all, the mission is not on the screen at all,
and the primary action is last and 40 px from the bottom edge on a phone.

### 5.3 Selection screens disagree with each other

* HERO and WORLD cards are **stadium-shaped** (fully rounded capsules); WEAPON
  cards are **rectangles**. Same flow, three shapes.
* HERO blurb text is the smallest body copy in the game and wraps to 5 lines in
  a 190 px column at 390x844.
* On WEAPON, 3 cards at ~200 px + a footer legend fill the entire screen with no
  header breathing room; on HERO, 3 cards leave a 200 px void under CONTINUE.
* `CONTINUE` is amber on all three selection screens, `LAUNCH` on WORLD; the
  flow's primary action changes name three times for one action (proceed).

### 5.4 HUD collisions at 390x844

* The ammo chip `PULSE 24/24` sits **under** the weapon-cycle button (measured in
  `gameplay-390x844.png`: the chip's right edge enters the button's left edge).
* The web shell's `SHOW CONTROLS (WEB)` bar covers the bottom-left corner where
  the movement stick's zone is, and is not part of the game's own layout.
* `SAFE ZONE · MOVE FREELY` and `NO HOSTILES` are two different status readouts
  stacked in one column with different type scales.

### 5.5 World composition

From `tour-1..4` and `gameplay`:

* The arena reads as **a grid of identical tiles** with no landmark inside it;
  the reactor is behind the camera from the player's default view.
* Structures are **flat-shaded slabs**: walls are single boxes with one albedo,
  so silhouettes are rectangles at every distance.
* No depth cue between near and far: fog density `0.007` over a 192 m world does
  almost nothing, and there is no mid-ground silhouette between the arena and
  the skyline.
* The orange runs (walls, ramps, platform rings) attract the eye more than the
  cyan reactor, i.e. **the wrong thing is the landmark**.

## 6. What this pass will not do

* **No new screens.** The journey BOOT → … → DEPLOY stays as it is.
* **No fake data.** Hero selection keeps claiming no stats until a hero stat
  model exists.
* **No gameplay rewrite.** Combat, controller, waves and the weapon contract are
  out of scope; the regression gate is 26/26 smoke tests plus a real browser run.
* **No art that only exists as a file.** Every texture this pass adds is loaded
  by a scene and photographed from the shipped payload, or it does not ship.

## 7. What the capture instrument was getting wrong

Everything above was measured with `tools/qa_flow_shots.mjs`. Two of its
measurements were reported correctly and explained incorrectly, and both were
found by fixing the tool and re-shooting:

* **LOADING vs CINEMATIC.** The tool waited `--settle` (6 s) *after* a stage
  announced itself, then photographed. A web build finishes a preload in one
  frame, so by the shutter LOADING had advanced to CINEMATIC and both files were
  the same screen. Transient stages are now photographed as soon as they draw a
  non-blank frame, and the manifest records `advancedTo` if the front end left
  before the shot. The underlying defect -- two stages sharing one composition
  -- was real and is fixed in the product, not in the tool.
* **"Same size, same mean, so same screen."** Image statistics cannot tell two
  screens apart when both are a photograph of the same art. The instrument now
  reports what stage the engine says it is on, and a stage that renders but does
  not match its name is a warning rather than a silent pass.

What the instrument cannot do, and this pass does not pretend otherwise:

* it photographs one viewport per page load (a browser build cannot rotate), so
  the rotation cases come from `tests/menu_layout_smoke.gd`, which resizes a live
  SubViewport instead;
* it measures pixels, not meaning. The dashboard shipped a duplicated caption
  under every stat for one commit because both copies fitted, were within the
  viewport, and passed every assertion. The capture caught it; the suite could
  not. That is why the visual pass stays in the loop after the tests go green.
