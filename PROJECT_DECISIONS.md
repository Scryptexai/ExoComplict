## Current development boundary

The current implementation slice is a Relay Yard greybox, third-person movement/camera, camera-center aiming, a respawning training target, and a capped Wave Director with deterministic Runner/Shooter composition. Combat uses shared `WeaponDefinition`/`WeaponController` resources for the Pulse Rifle, Nova Launcher, and Arc Shooter; Arc chains to at most two visible targets within a 5-unit hop radius with 0.7 damage per hop. Shooter uses a range-holding state, a visible locked-aim windup, and a cover-blocked attack; both archetypes share health/death signals. Player health/retry, hit/damage feedback, and pause/reset are wired. Active spawns are capped and validated; seven headless smoke tests pass, while arena geometry, enemy statistics, and wave pacing remain provisional. The deck/ramp, cover routes, and enemy navigation still need interactive validation. Run-scoped `RunStats`/`RewardService` records Runner/Shooter eliminations, Run Score, BP earned, and survival time; the Results/retry overlay reads those actual values. Persistent BP wallet, unlock/equip, save, and final art are not implemented. It is **not** the completed MVP loop.
